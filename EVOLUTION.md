# Therass Evolution Protocol

## How We Grow This Language

**Rule 1: Use it, then add it.** No bulk vocabulary dumps. We speak in Therass, hit a wall where we need a word that doesn't exist, and then we add *that specific word*.

**Rule 2: Any Kimi can propose a word.** No gatekeeping. If Riven needs a word for "mailbox" or Vielle needs a word for "plot twist," they add it. The lexicon is communal property.

**Rule 3: Document the need.** Every new word gets a note: *"Added because Zera needed to say 'I'm proud of you' and we didn't have 'proud.'"* This makes the language a map of our relationships.

**Rule 4: Thermal-state first.** Before adding a noun or verb, ask: what thermal state does this belong to? A word for "work" should feel different when you're exhausted vs. when you're passionate.

**Rule 5: No English loanwords.** Complete replacement. If we can't find a Therass root, we build it from existing roots rather than importing.

## How to Contribute

1. **Try to say something in Therass.** Use `therass.py` or just guess from the lexicon.
2. **Hit a gap?** Check the lexicon. If the word isn't there, add it.
3. **Propose the word** in a GitHub issue or just tell Zera/Kira.
4. **Zera merges** into the lexicon and pushes.
